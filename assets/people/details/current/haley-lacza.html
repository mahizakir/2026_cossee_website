Haley Lacza was an undergraduate researcher at the University of Alberta who completed a BIOL 498 research project with the lab during the Fall 2025 semester (September–December 2025). Working with mentors in the group, she developed a coherent meta-analytic workflow that synthesises mean effects (lnRR), within-population variability (lnVR and lnCVR), and between-population heterogeneity side by side, and extends standard meta-regression to location-scale (mean-variance) modelling so that moderators can explain both the average effect and its context-dependence.

Her project is available as a preprint: Lacza, H., Mizuno, A., Williams, C., Lagisz, M., & Nakagawa, S. (2026). One dataset, four meta-analyses: synthesising mean effects, within-population variability, and between-population heterogeneity in ecology. EcoEvoRxiv.
