Christin Manthey [she/her/hers]

Role: Postdoc
Country of origin: Germany

I am fascinated by the evolutionary processes that made insects the most diverse group of animals on Earth. My research explores questions such as: Why did complete metamorphosis evolve? What shapes global patterns of insect biodiversity? And how have microbial symbionts influenced insect evolution? To answer these questions, I combine large-scale comparative analyses, phylogenetic methods, and comparative genomics.
