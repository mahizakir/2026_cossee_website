I joined the Collaboration for Open Science and Synthesis in Ecology and Evolution Lab in January 2026 as a Master's student. My research focuses on two interconnected areas: ecological small area estimation and statistical methodology.

My applied work aims to provide reliable ecological estimates from sparse and incomplete observations, with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification and informing ecological decision-making. On the methodological side, I develop statistical frameworks for mixed-effects models, drawing on information geometry to study statistical inference.

Before joining the lab, I studied Statistics at the University of Toronto, with a focus on mathematical and theoretical statistics.
