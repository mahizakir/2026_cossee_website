How to get to COSSEE

There are several ways to reach COSSEE (CW312) once you are on campus. Here is one recommended route.

  1. Go to the CCIS (Centennial Centre for Interdisciplinary Science) building.
  2. Enter the building and walk straight ahead.
  3. Continue straight and go down a small set of stairs.
  4. After the stairs, keep walking straight.
  5. You will see a café on your left and enter an open area.
  6. The Biological Sciences (BioSci) building entrance will be in front of you.
  7. Enter the building. You will see elevators on your right and stairs further ahead.
  8. Take the stairs up one floor.
  9. At the top of the stairs, you will see two doors ahead, one on the left and one on the right.
  10. Enter through the door on the right.
  11. Walk straight to the end of the hallway.
  12. Turn left. The COSSEE will be on your right :)
