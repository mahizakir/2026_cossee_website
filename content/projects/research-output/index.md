We invite all current lab members to record their COSSEE research output through [researchoutput.cossee.org](https://researchoutput.cossee.org/).

Please log in with your UAlberta email account.

You will already see your publications recorded on the platform if they are linked through ORCID. At the moment, only ORCID records are preloaded.

This consolidated database will be used for COSSEE administrative reporting purposes.
