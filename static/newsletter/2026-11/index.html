Synthesis & open science

1. The revised Tri-Agency open access policy is out: no embargo for grants from 2027

CIHR, NSERC and SSHRC, "Tri-Agency Open Access Policy on Publications (2026)". Policy text · agency page · UVic Libraries summary, 15 October 2026

Released in October (UVic Libraries gives the date as 5 October), it takes effect on 1 January 2027 for grants and Chairs awarded on or after that date. Peer-reviewed articles must be freely available in a Canadian institutional repository or trusted Canadian platform at the time of publication, with no embargo, even when the journal is already open access. Either the accepted manuscript or the published version will do.

Authors must keep enough rights to apply an open licence, such as Creative Commons, tell the publisher about these obligations at submission, and cite the funder and grant number in the article. Student and postdoctoral awards are not covered, though holders are encouraged to comply by no-cost routes. Awards made before 2027 stay under the 2015 policy and its 12-month window.

No embargo means choosing the journal and licence at submission, and telling the publisher then. It applies to grants awarded from 2027 onward, not to existing ones. It closes one of the two items in last issue's Policy watch; the other is in §06.

2. Global biodiversity models predict poorly at local scales

Nyström, Smith, Mandle, Gonzalez, Schön & Andermann, "Global biodiversity models exhibit low predictive accuracy on local scales", Nature Ecology & Evolution, 10 November 2026, open access. 10.1038/s41559-026-03205-z

According to the abstract, ecologically structured models interpolate better than study-centric mixed-effects models, but both perform poorly when extrapolating. We have read the abstract only.

Study-level mixed models are the backbone of our meta-analyses; this is a direct test of how far their predictions travel.

3. Bioengineered retracts 119 more papers in a paper-mill investigation

Retraction Watch, "Bioengineered retracts 119 more papers as investigation continues", 3 November 2026. retractionwatch.com

The Taylor & Francis journal retracted the batch in October. Concerns date to 2021, and the journal was delisted from Web of Science in May 2025.

A reminder to check the retraction status of included studies at the end of a synthesis, not only at screening.

4. Biodiversity safeguards ecosystem services and functions worldwide

Moffett et al., "Biodiversity safeguards ecosystem services and functions worldwide", Nature Ecology & Evolution, 6 October 2026, open access. 10.1038/s41559-026-03200-4

423 studies, 1,959 datasets and 222,829 data points, according to the Methods.

A large biodiversity–function synthesis; worth checking how it handles non-independence across more than 200,000 data points from 423 studies.
